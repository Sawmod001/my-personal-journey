# Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) - Review Questions

## 1. What is SDLC?
The Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) is a structured process used to plan, design, develop, test, deploy, and maintain software systems.

## 2. Why is SDLC important?
SDLC provides a systematic approach to software development, helping teams deliver quality products on time, within budget, and with reduced risks.

## 3. List the seven phases of SDLC.
1. Planning
2. Requirements Analysis
3. System Design
4. Development (Coding)
5. Testing
6. Deployment
7. Maintenance

## 4. Differentiate between functional and non-functional requirements.

### Functional Requirements
These describe what the system should do, such as user registration, login, and payment processing.

### Non-Functional Requirements
These define how the system should perform, such as security, reliability, speed, and scalability.

## 5. What happens during the testing phase?
During testing, developers and testers identify and fix defects, verify functionality, and ensure the software meets specified requirements.

## 6. Explain the difference between deployment and maintenance.

### Deployment
Deployment involves releasing the software to users or a production environment.

### Maintenance
Maintenance involves updating, improving, and fixing issues in the software after deployment.

## 7. Compare the Waterfall and Agile models.

| Waterfall | Agile |
|------------|---------|
| Sequential approach | Iterative approach |
| Changes are difficult to implement | Changes can be accommodated easily |
| Testing occurs after development | Testing occurs continuously |
| Suitable for fixed requirements | Suitable for changing requirements |

## 8. Who are the major stakeholders involved in SDLC?
Major stakeholders include:
- Clients/Customers
- Project Managers
- Business Analysts
- Software Developers
- Testers/QA Engineers
- End Users

## 9. What is User Acceptance Testing (UAT)?
User Acceptance Testing (UAT) is the final testing stage where end users verify that the software meets their needs and business requirements before release.

## 10. Why is maintenance important after deployment?
Maintenance ensures the software remains functional, secure, up-to-date, and capable of meeting evolving user and business needs.
